Kickstart Consulting

Fixing the first rung on the career ladder.

Our mission

We exist to kickstart the careers of high potential young people searching for their first professional role in an impossible job market.

What we do

We design and support our clients to implement entry level employment programs. This includes our:

  • Careful calibration of the role of entry level staff to the organisational needs of our clients, including the design of position descriptions, workflows and development roadmaps
  • Recruitment of top emerging talent into these roles
  • Development of entry level staff through training modules and coaching

Our theory of change

For early career professionals

  1. 1

    Reserving a set of paid positions for people with no previous experience protects the first rung on the career ladder, creating a clear entry point into an industry within an impossible job market.

  2. 2

    A relatively small amount of paid work in a professional role each week can make a significant difference to kickstarting a professional’s career. A first paid role helps to build practical skills, a CV, professional networks and references. This strengthens the position of early career professionals when applying for future opportunities.

  3. 3

    Short fixed-term placements fit flexibly around young people’s university, study and other paid work commitments, making participation more accessible.

For our clients

  1. 1

    Entry level roles used to serve the purpose of completing manual grunt work before these tasks became automated by AI. The automation of these roles offers organisations significant efficiency gains that they are obligated to not ignore.

  2. 2

    Organisations need to continue employing entry level staff in order to build pipelines of future talent. Organisations also benefit from the cost efficiency of hiring new recruits who are less expensive than more seasoned professionals.

  3. 3

    The productive employment of early career professionals in the age of AI requires the redesign of entry level roles. The positions must adapt from focusing on the completion of newly automated administrative grunt work to activities that make a productive contribution to the organisation while being capable of being completed by people with no previous experience.

  4. 4

    Kickstart has a proven track record of successfully calibrating these entry level roles to productive outputs through careful program design, selection of high potential candidates and training designed specifically for new workforce entrants.

Why the Kickstart model works

Preserves the first rung on the career ladder

We reserve roles specifically for candidates with no previous experience.

Designed for real schedules

Short, flexible hours can work alongside university, study and other paid employment, making it possible for busy young people to get their first paid professional work in the industry of their choice.

Low-cost, rinse and repeat program for employers

After we design an entry level program for a client, employers can easily continue to place new talent into these positions every 3–6 months.

Fill future vacancies immediately

Our programs create pools of trusted talent that our clients can draw on to fill future vacancies, helping to streamline their recruitment.
